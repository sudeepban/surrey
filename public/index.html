🚲 Surrey Lobby

5 seats: a front basket, a bench of 3, and one out back. Get in one. Push someone out if you have to — the dog in the carrier isn't going anywhere.

Walk up to the bike and press E to sit. Stand next to a taken seat and press E to push them off.

🚲💨 The surrey rolls out!

All 5 seats locked in (plus one very good dog). Ride on.
